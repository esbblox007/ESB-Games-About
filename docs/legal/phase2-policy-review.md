## Cross-policy consistency rules applied

1. Draft or noindex documents are not incorporated into the Terms merely because their title is mentioned.
2. Privacy notices do not treat acceptance of Terms as consent for all personal-data processing.
3. Support and Careers acknowledgements are notices/confirmations, not blanket consent for processing that relies on another lawful basis.
4. Mandatory consumer, refund, cancellation, privacy, child-safety and court/regulator rights override conflicting contractual wording.
5. ESB Games does not use one universal 13+ rule for every legal purpose; age, parental involvement and feature eligibility depend on law and risk.
6. ESBucks are treated as transferable virtual platform value rather than being marketed as a bank account, legal tender or investment; creator payout eligibility is separated from ordinary transferable balances.
7. Draft revenue shares, payout rates, prices, benefit promises, compliance badges, SLAs and certification claims are not treated as commitments.
8. Creator import tools do not grant rights to third-party Roblox or other-platform assets; creators remain responsible for licences and permissions.
9. Significant moderation should be proportionate, auditable and reviewable where required or appropriate, without promising disclosure that could create safety or privacy risk.
10. Privacy retention is expressed using criteria until ESB Games approves an operational retention schedule that the systems can actually enforce.
11. Optional marketing, analytics or advertising consent is separated from processing necessary to provide or secure a service.
12. Family linking does not automatically expose a child's private messages or safety records to a parent or guardian.
13. Content ratings are advisory by default, but cannot override a legal or platform-safety requirement to prevent access to particular content or features.
14. Google Translate is not loaded merely because of browser locale; third-party translation is tied to an explicit or saved language choice.

## Business decisions confirmed 23 August 2026

### Legal structure
- The intended operating/contracting company is **ESB Games**.
- A parent company is planned, but the parent is not currently intended to replace ESB Games as the ordinary customer-facing contracting entity merely because it owns ESB Games.
- Exact incorporated legal name, company number, registered office and group structure are still to be supplied after incorporation/structure is final.
- No Data Protection Officer, UK representative or EU representative is currently formally appointed. Legal review must determine whether any appointment is required before publication.
- Governing law remains undecided. Current working recommendation for review is England and Wales, subject to incorporation location, launch countries and qualified legal advice, while preserving mandatory local consumer rights.

### Public launch commercial scope
ESB Games currently intends all of the following to be available at public launch, subject to production and legal readiness:
- real-money purchases;
- ESBucks purchases;
- ESB Games gift cards;
- subscriptions;
- Marketplace purchases; and
- creator payouts.

### ESBucks and gift cards
- ESBucks **never expire**, whether obtained through a gift card, purchased directly or earned.
- ESB Games gift cards are bought using real-world money and **never expire**.
- ESB Games will not impose dormancy fees solely because ESBucks or a gift card remain unused.
- Peer-to-peer ESBucks transfers are intended to be allowed.
- Small ordinary transfers may complete immediately; higher-value or unusual transfers may enter a Pending/review state under risk controls.
- Every ESBucks movement must be traceable through an auditable ledger.
- User-facing economic source categories are **Gift Card**, **Purchased** and **Earned**. Refunds, corrections and reversals are audit events rather than a fourth economic source.
- A peer-to-peer transfer must preserve source lineage rather than erasing where value came from.
- Ordinary transferable ESBucks are not automatically cash-withdrawable. Qualifying creator proceeds must be tracked separately for creator payout eligibility.
- A UK payments/e-money regulatory-perimeter review is a hard launch gate for purchased value + peer-to-peer transfer + Marketplace use. Traceability is a control, not a substitute for required FCA authorisation, registration, exclusion or notification analysis.

### Creator ownership
- Creator projects may be owned by eligible **individual creators** or eligible **verified groups/organisations**.
- Ownership transfers should be authorised and auditable.
- Collaboration permission does not itself transfer ownership.

### Appeals
- ESB Games will not promise one universal public appeal-response SLA at launch.
- Appeals should be handled as promptly as reasonably possible, with any legal deadlines taking priority.

### Age ratings and content guidance
- ESB Games will use its own age suggestions/content descriptors initially rather than claim PEGI, ESRB or another external certification that has not been obtained.
- Ratings are **advisory by default**. For ordinary content, a label such as 18+ does not automatically mean the platform hard-blocks every younger account solely because of the label.
- This advisory model cannot override law or safety requirements. Where a category requires effective child-access prevention or a feature has a mandatory eligibility rule, ESB Games may apply hard age assurance/access controls.
- A rating never authorises content prohibited by the Community Standards.
