# ESB Games Phase 2 — Legal & Privacy Review Register

Status: **Internal review only — no policy in this Phase 2 branch is approved for legal effect solely by being drafted here.**

## Publication rule

`lib/content/policy-publication.ts` remains fail-closed with an empty published set. Policy review pages are `noindex` until a document is explicitly approved for publication.

## Cross-policy consistency rules applied

1. Draft or noindex documents are not incorporated into the Terms merely because their title is mentioned.
2. Privacy notices do not treat acceptance of Terms as consent for all personal-data processing.
3. Support and Careers acknowledgements are notices/confirmations, not blanket consent for processing that relies on another lawful basis.
4. Mandatory consumer, refund, cancellation, privacy, child-safety and court/regulator rights override conflicting contractual wording.
5. ESB Games does not use one universal 13+ rule for every legal purpose; age, parental involvement and feature eligibility depend on law and risk.
6. ESBucks are treated as virtual platform value, not legal tender or a bank deposit, and creator payout eligibility is separated from ordinary balances.
7. Draft revenue shares, payout rates, prices, benefit promises, compliance badges, SLAs and certification claims are not treated as commitments.
8. Creator import tools do not grant rights to third-party Roblox or other-platform assets; creators remain responsible for licences and permissions.
9. Significant moderation should be proportionate, auditable and reviewable where required or appropriate, without promising disclosure that could create safety or privacy risk.
10. Privacy retention is expressed using criteria until ESB Games approves an operational retention schedule that the systems can actually enforce.
11. Optional marketing, analytics or advertising consent is separated from processing necessary to provide or secure a service.
12. Family linking does not automatically expose a child's private messages or safety records to a parent or guardian.

## Decisions required from ESB Games before final publication

### A. Legal identity and dispute framework — blocks Terms and main Privacy Policy
- Confirm the exact legal contracting/controller entity name.
- Confirm registered/service address to publish.
- Confirm principal governing law and jurisdiction wording.
- Confirm whether a UK/EU representative or Data Protection Officer is legally required and, if so, details to publish.

### B. Production privacy governance — blocks main Privacy Policy finalisation
- Approve production vendor/subprocessor register.
- Approve international transfer map and safeguards.
- Approve lawful-basis register by processing purpose.
- Approve retention schedule for accounts, security logs, Support, Trust & Safety, appeals, payments, creator data, Family Centre, Careers, backups and legal holds.

### C. Commercial model — blocks payment/subscription/ESBucks policies becoming operative
- Confirm seller / merchant-of-record model.
- Confirm payment processors and supported purchase channels.
- Confirm whether subscriptions are direct, app-store, or both; trial/annual plan intentions; launch countries.
- Confirm whether purchased ESBucks expire.
- Confirm gifting/transfer rules.
- Confirm creator payout eligibility and whether any cash conversion rate will be publicly promised.

### D. Creator model
- Confirm who can legally own creator projects: individuals, organisations/groups, or both.
- Confirm publishing eligibility and review stages.
- Confirm monetisation and Marketplace launch status.
- Confirm plugin/API terms before those features are public.

### E. Safety operations
- Confirm appeal deadlines by decision type if ESB Games wants fixed deadlines.
- Confirm external child-safety reporting process and escalation ownership.
- Confirm the age/content rating framework adopted for launch countries.

### F. Copyright
- Confirm launch jurisdictions and whether any statutory copyright-agent registration or jurisdiction-specific notice process is required before publishing the Copyright Policy.

## Recommended publication order after decisions and legal review

1. Privacy Policy + Support Privacy Notice + Careers Privacy Notice
2. Terms of Service + Community Standards
3. Child Safety + Reporting & Enforcement + Appeals
4. Cookie Policy
5. Creator Terms + UGC Guidelines + Copyright/Trademark
6. Payment/Refund/Subscription/ESBucks policies immediately before the applicable paid features become available
7. Remaining guides and supporting policies

## Final release gate

Before any policy is moved to the published set:
- factual review against the current product and backend;
- legal/entity decisions above resolved;
- external qualified legal review recommended for the binding Terms, consumer payment/subscription rules, privacy framework and child-safety obligations;
- links and aliases tested in production;
- effective date set to the true publication/effective date, not backdated;
- `robots` and sitemap behaviour verified;
- published policy text version recorded for audit purposes.
