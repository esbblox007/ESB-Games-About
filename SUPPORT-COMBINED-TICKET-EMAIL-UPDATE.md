# Combined guest support email

Guest ticket creation now sends no immediate standalone confirmation email. When the guest selects **Send verification code**, one Resend email contains:

- confirmation that the ticket was created;
- the ticket reference;
- the private ticket link;
- the six-digit one-time code;
- the three-minute expiry and security guidance.

Account-linked tickets continue to receive their normal creation notification because they do not use guest email verification.
